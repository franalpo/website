about

I'm franc alvarez, a former Rope Access Level III technician, current estimator, returning student, photographer, cyclist, and writer based in Albuquerque, New Mexico.

My background has taken me through industrial inspection, construction, drafting, field observation, and now estimating. I spent years working in high-access environments before choosing a slower, more grounded life focused on learning, community, and building things that last.

This site is a place for my projects, photographs, writing, maps, experiments, and notes. Some of it is professional. Some of it is personal. Most of it is connected by curiosity, place, and the ongoing attempt to build a life I don't need to escape from.
